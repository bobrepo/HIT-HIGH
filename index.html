Clear Answers

Common questions before you start

Transparent answers to what prospective clients ask us most often.

What do I need to prepare before reaching out?
Just an idea of what your business does and what problem you want solved. You don't need wireframes or technical specs. During our initial conversation, we ask targeted questions to help define the exact scope, pages, and features.
How do you determine timeline and cost?
Every project is scoped individually based on features, database depth, and third-party integrations (like payment gateways or hardware printers). Standard business websites typically launch in 2 to 3 weeks, while comprehensive web apps or billing engines take 4 to 8 weeks. After our first talk, you receive a transparent fixed milestone proposal.
Can you build software that fits our current shop or office workflow?
Yes, absolutely. That is our core advantage over ready-made software. Whether you track stock in physical ledgers, invoice clients on paper, or manage jobs via WhatsApp, we tailor the software interface to match your staff's existing habits so adoption is frictionless.
Do you work with international clients outside India?
Yes. HITHIGH provides global service. We coordinate remotely across time zones via WhatsApp, Google Meet, and email, with digital milestone previews and secure international payment support.
Can non-technical staff manage or update content after launch?
Yes. We provide clean, simple administrative panels where you can edit text, upload product photos, view customer invoices, and adjust pricing without touching a single line of code. We also provide full training and ongoing support.
